Why Deterministic Rule-Based Expert Systems for Emergency Guidance?

In life-or-death disaster situations, non-deterministic generative models present grave risks: hallucinated phone numbers, speculative evacuation advice, and high network latency. DisasterShield employs a pure deterministic decision-tree rule engine:

100% Deterministic & Transparent Every guidance outcome maps to a verified statutory safety path with unambiguous, auditable decision branches.
100% Offline Capable Zero dependency on internet connectivity, external cloud APIs, or paid token services during active infrastructure failures.
Zero Hallucinated Hotlines Exclusively references verified official national emergency helplines: 112 (ERSS), 1078 (NDRF), 101 (Fire), and 108 (Ambulance).
Multi-Level Urgency Triage Dynamically classifies user situations across Normal, Precautionary, Urgent, and Critical severity levels with immediate action directives.

Academic & Public Safety Statement

This application is constructed for college coursework demonstrating deterministic rule-based expert systems in crisis guidance. In an actual active emergency, users must always follow direct orders issued on ground by the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF), local police, fire service, and district administration. No automated tool replaces direct civil defense directives.
